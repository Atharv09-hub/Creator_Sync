# Persona 1 – Creative Organizer

## Persona Snapshot

**Name:** Maya Kapoor

- **Age:** 27
- **Gender:** Female
- **Location:** Bandra, Mumbai
- **Occupation:** Freelance Content Creator / Social Media Strategist

---

## Background

Maya is a freelance content creator who manages writing, video planning, and creative projects for lifestyle brands. She works from home and juggles multiple campaigns, deadlines, and content ideas each week.

### Primary Goals

- Keep content planning, production, and analytics organized in one place
- Track deadlines and progress across writing, video, and social media workflows
- Use smart tools to minimize manual work and avoid missed uploads

### Secondary Goals

- Store reusable media, scripts, and creative assets securely
- Maintain healthy habits and focus with a structured workflow
- Get quick insights into performance so she can adjust content fast

### Needs and Expectations

- A clean, intuitive dashboard that shows project status at a glance
- Clear task boards that help her move ideas from concept to publish
- Reliable storage for draft scripts, assets, and campaign notes
- A safe place to manage and revisit creative routines and habits

### Pain Points

- Losing track of content ideas when working on many campaigns
- Switching between too many apps for planning, scripts, and analytics
- Missing deadlines because tasks are scattered or poorly prioritized
- Wasting time on manual tracking instead of creating

### User Quote

"I want one workspace that helps me plan, publish, and improve without losing my creative flow."

# Persona 2 – Operations Lead

## Persona Snapshot

**Name:** Arjun Desai

- **Age:** 32
- **Gender:** Male
- **Location:** Pune, Maharashtra
- **Occupation:** Operations Manager at a small creative agency

## Background

Arjun oversees project delivery and resource coordination for a team of designers, writers, and video editors. He needs a dependable way to monitor progress, assign tasks, and ensure every project stays on schedule.

### Primary Goals

- Keep all team tasks and deadlines visible in one workspace
- Quickly identify bottlenecks and reassign work when needed
- Share updates with stakeholders without wasting time in meetings

### Secondary Goals

- Maintain central access to campaign scripts, creative assets, and client notes
- Reduce reliance on email and spreadsheets for task tracking
- Improve accountability through transparent workflow stages

### Needs and Expectations

- A Kanban-style board for tracking work by status
- A shared media vault for assets and reusable content
- Easy collaboration tools for comments and handoffs
- A dashboard summary for high-level project health

### Pain Points

- Team updates are scattered across chat, email, and documents
- Important deadlines slip when tasks are not visible or prioritized
- Clients ask for progress reports that are hard to assemble quickly
- Creative work becomes inefficient without a structured workflow

### User Quote

"I need a single system that shows where every project stands and helps our team stay aligned."

# Persona 3 – Independent Creator

## Persona Snapshot

**Name:** Priya Nair

- **Age:** 24
- **Gender:** Female
- **Location:** Kochi, Kerala
- **Occupation:** Independent scriptwriter and lifestyle blogger

## Background

Priya creates content and story ideas for blogs, short films, and paid brand collaborations. She balances writing with research, editing, and maintaining an online presence, so she values a workspace that simplifies her creative process.

### Primary Goals

- Capture ideas and turn them into completed scripts and posts
- Track daily habits and creative routines that support productivity
- Manage assets, drafts, and deadlines without losing momentum

### Secondary Goals

- Keep a library of reusable templates and assets for faster creation
- Use analytics and progress tracking to stay motivated
- Feel confident that her work is secure and easy to revisit

### Needs and Expectations

- Simple habit tracking tied to creative output
- Smart organization for script drafts, images, and video concepts
- A clean interface that avoids distraction
- Fast access to recent work and project summaries

### Pain Points

- Creative ideas get forgotten without a reliable capture system
- Switching tools steals time from writing and editing
- Habits and progress feel disconnected from actual project work
- Storing and finding assets becomes messy over time

### User Quote

"I want a creative workspace that keeps my ideas, tasks, and habits together so I can stay focused."
